Crowns Privacy Policy

Effective 27 October 2026

Crowns is a logic puzzle game. This policy explains what information the game handles and why. In short: your progress is saved on your device. The only things that leave it are leaderboard scores and, in the Google Play version, a backup of your progress. Which service receives them depends on where you got the game:

  • From Google Play (Android): Google Play Games, if you sign in to it.
  • Everywhere else (Windows, Linux, the browser version, and the Android version from itch.io or GitHub): SilentWolf, unless you turn it off in Settings. These versions contain no Google services.

Information stored on your device

Crowns saves the following on your device so you can pick up where you left off:

  • Puzzles you have solved, your best times and boards in progress
  • Daily puzzle streaks and other stats
  • Progress in The Climb
  • Your settings, such as dark mode, language and the daily reminder time

This information is deleted when you clear the game's data or uninstall it. The daily reminder is a notification scheduled on your device; nothing is sent anywhere to deliver it.

Google Play Games (Google Play version)

The version of Crowns from Google Play uses Google Play Games Services for leaderboards, achievements and cloud saves. It does not use SilentWolf. If you sign in to Play Games, the game sends Google:

  • Your times on the daily puzzle and the weekly challenge
  • Your best run in The Climb
  • The number of numbered puzzles you have solved
  • The achievements you unlock
  • A copy of your progress (solved puzzles, times, stats and boards in progress, but not your settings), so it can be restored on another device

Google links this information to your Play Games profile. Leaderboard scores and achievements are shown to other players according to your Play Games visibility settings. Google processes this information under the Google Privacy Policy. You can manage or delete your Play Games profile and its data, including saved games, in the Google Play Games settings.

Signing in is optional. If you do not sign in, nothing is sent to Google and the rest of the game works the same.

SilentWolf (all other versions)

The Windows, Linux and browser versions, and the Android version from itch.io or GitHub, use SilentWolf for their online leaderboards. When you solve a puzzle that has a leaderboard, the game sends SilentWolf:

  • Your time on the daily puzzle or the weekly challenge, your best run in The Climb, or the number of numbered puzzles you have solved
  • The leaderboard name shown on the Leaderboards screen. It starts as "Player" and a random number, and you can change it to anything you like.
  • A random ID made by the game, so that your scores are kept together even if you change your name or someone else picks the same one. It is not linked to any account or device identifier.

Your leaderboard name and scores are shown publicly to other players. Choose a name that doesn't identify you if you'd rather stay anonymous. SilentWolf stores the scores on its servers. To have your scores removed, contact us at the address below with your leaderboard name.

You can stop sending scores to SilentWolf at any time by turning off Online leaderboards (SilentWolf) in Settings.

Deleting your data

To have your Crowns leaderboard scores deleted from SilentWolf:

  1. Open Crowns, go to Leaderboards and note the name shown under Your name.
  2. Email ascteroido@protonmail.com with the subject "Crowns data deletion" and that leaderboard name.

All scores under that name are removed from every Crowns leaderboard on SilentWolf within 30 days. Nothing is kept afterwards. The game keeps no other data about you on any server.

Everything else is in your control directly. Progress saved on your device is deleted when you clear the game's data or uninstall it. In the Google Play version, scores, achievements and the cloud save in Google Play Games are deleted from your Play Games settings.

What Crowns does not do

  • No advertising and no analytics or tracking
  • No accounts of its own; we do not ask for your email address or other personal details
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone
  • No selling or sharing of your information

The game uses the internet only for the services described above.

Children

Crowns does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Leaderboard scores are handled by Google Play Games and SilentWolf as described above.

Changes to this policy
