Crowns Privacy Policy

Effective 24 October 2026

Crowns is a logic puzzle game. This policy explains what information the app handles and why. In short: your progress stays on your device, and the only thing that leaves it is your leaderboard scores, sent to Google Play Games if you choose to sign in.

Information stored on your device

Crowns saves the following on your device so you can pick up where you left off:

  • Puzzles you have solved, your best times and boards in progress
  • Daily puzzle streaks and other stats
  • Progress in The Climb
  • Your settings, such as dark mode and region patterns

This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Google Play Games

On Android, Crowns uses Google Play Games Services for its global leaderboards. If you sign in to Play Games, the app submits these scores to Google:

  • Your time on the daily puzzle
  • Your best run in The Climb
  • The number of numbered puzzles you have solved

Google links these scores to your Play Games profile and shows them on leaderboards to other players, according to your Play Games visibility settings. Google processes this information under the Google Privacy Policy. You can manage or delete your Play Games profile and its data in the Google Play Games settings.

Signing in is optional. If you do not sign in, no scores are submitted and the rest of the game works the same.

What Crowns does not do

  • No advertising and no analytics or tracking
  • No accounts of its own; we do not ask for your name, email address or other personal details
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone
  • No selling or sharing of your information

The app's internet permission is used only to talk to Google Play Games.

Children

Crowns does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Leaderboard scores are handled by Google Play Games as described above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
